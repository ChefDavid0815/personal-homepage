# ChefZC — personal space

> Inferred from the owner's brief on 2026-10-10 (the owner asked for full creative freedom and no questionnaire). Assumptions are marked.

## What it is
The personal website of ChefZC, a Year 12 IB student at GEMS Wellington International School in Dubai. It holds his profile, a gallery of real software and web projects, a school lab, model studies, a game room, a timeline (Now), bilingual journal posts, a usage observatory and a flow index. Every page is bilingual (中 / EN).

## Who it is for
- Visitors meeting ChefZC for the first time: schools, competition judges, collaborators, friends. (assumption)
- Award juries: the owner's stated goal is an award-level visual work.
- ChefZC himself, as a living record that keeps growing.

## Hard rules
- No original information may be lost. Content can be restructured, moved and restyled freely.
- Each project exhibit keeps its own base visual language; the shell around it may change.
- Real facts only: no invented claims, numbers or projects.
- Bilingual parity, keyboard access, reduced-motion and the site-wide motion switch are respected everywhere.
